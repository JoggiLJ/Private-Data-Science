# Statistics Concept Inventory (SCI)

**Source:** `SCI.pdf` — a statistics concept inventory (introductory-statistics education research instrument, in the same family as FCI/BCI/CINS).

**What it tests:** Conceptual understanding of introductory statistics — e.g. choosing the correct test statistic, reasoning about sampling and probability, and correctly interpreting percentiles/statistical claims — rather than mechanical calculation.

**Format:** 21 multiple-choice items, tagged by `topic_area` (e.g. "Inferential," "Probability").

**What's in this folder:** `SCI.pdf` (source) and `sci_item_bank.json` (structured items).
