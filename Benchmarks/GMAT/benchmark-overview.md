# GMAT

**Source:** Official GMAT practice/sample questions, transcribed from screenshots (`Screenshots/`, combined into `GMAT_combined.pdf`).

**What it tests:** Graduate Management Admission Test quantitative reasoning — word problems covering arithmetic, algebra, combinatorics, probability, and geometry, at the difficulty level used for business-school admissions.

**Format:** 56 multiple-choice items (5 options, A–E), each with a `word_count` field.

**What's in this folder:** `gmat_items.json` (structured items) and `Screenshots/` (the original question screenshots plus a combined PDF).
