# LSAT Logical Reasoning

**Source:** Official, publicly disclosed LSAT sections — Jan 2023, Feb 2024, and Apr 2025 disclosure booklets (`Disclosure-2024.pdf`, `Disclosure 2025_accessible.pdf`, `January-2023-LSAT-Disclosure-Booklet.pdf`).

**What it tests:** LSAT "Logical Reasoning" — short argument passages followed by a question (e.g. strongest counter, assumption, flaw), testing informal logical/argumentative reasoning rather than formal symbolic logic.

**Format:** 101 items with official answer keys, 5 options (A–E) each, tagged with `word_count`.

**What's in this folder:** `lsat_lr_items.json` / `lsat_lr_items.txt` (structured and plain-text question sets) and the three source disclosure PDFs.
