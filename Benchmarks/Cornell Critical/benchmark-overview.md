# Cornell Conditional Reasoning Test

**Source:** Cornell conditional-reasoning instrument associated with the Cornell Critical Thinking Tests (Ennis et al.); sourced here from `CornellConditionalReas.pdf` and an ERIC-archived form (`ED003818.pdf` / `ED003818_Table.pdf`).

**What it tests:** Whether a respondent can correctly evaluate conditional ("if P, then Q") statements — i.e., distinguishing logically valid inferences (modus ponens, modus tollens) from classic invalid ones (affirming the consequent, denying the antecedent). Each item gives a conditional premise plus a fact, then asks whether a conclusion is "Yes" (true), "No" (false), or "Maybe" (indeterminate) — the three-way answer format is itself part of what's being tested, since picking "Maybe" correctly on invalid-inference items is the key discriminator.

**Format:** 72 items, tagged by `group` (1–4, corresponding to the reasoning-pattern type) and `form` (e.g. "CF1", the specific test form/booklet).

**What's in this folder:** `cornell_reasoning_items.json` (structured items) and `cornell_conditional_reasoning_questions.txt` (plain-text version), source PDFs, and `Table.xlsx` (scoring/reference table from the source).
