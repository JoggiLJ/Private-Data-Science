# Conceptual Inventory of Natural Selection (CINS)

**Source:** Anderson, Fisher & Norman (2002), "Development and Evaluation of the Conceptual Inventory of Natural Selection," *Journal of Research in Science Teaching*, 39, 952–978.

**What it tests:** Understanding of natural selection and evolution, specifically whether students hold the correct mechanistic model (heritable variation, differential survival/reproduction, population-level change) versus common misconceptions (e.g., need-based or intentional adaptation). Built around three recurring scenarios: Galápagos finches, Venezuelan guppies, and Canary Island lizards.

**Format:** 20 multiple-choice items.

**What's in this folder:** `CINS.pdf` (the source paper) and `CINS.json` (20 transcribed items). Note: the source PDF doesn't print an answer key — the `correct_label` values in the JSON were AI-reasoned from natural-selection theory on 2026-07-06 and should be treated as a best-effort key, not an authoritative one.
