# Biological Concepts Instrument (BCI)

**Source:** Klymkowsky, Underwood & Garvin-Doxas (2010), "Biological Concepts Instrument (BCI.1)," arXiv:1012.4501.

**What it tests:** A concept inventory (not a knowledge-recall quiz) aimed at core biology misconceptions — things like randomness/stochastic processes in biological systems, energy flow, and cell behavior — rather than factual recall. Designed to surface conceptual misunderstandings that persist even among students who can pass ordinary biology exams.

**Format:** 29–30 multiple-choice items (one item, Q23, was dropped from the original 30-item trial version and later reinstated in the current LASSO-hosted version). Items are grouped into 6 concept clusters.

**What's in this folder:** `Biological_Concepts_Instrument.pdf` (the source paper) and `BCI_biological_concepts_instrument.json` (the 29 transcribed items with answer key and category labels, transcribed from the PDF on 2026-07-06). Note: a revised 30-item version is administered live via LASSO (lassoeducation.org) but is login-gated, so its exact item text isn't reproduced here.
