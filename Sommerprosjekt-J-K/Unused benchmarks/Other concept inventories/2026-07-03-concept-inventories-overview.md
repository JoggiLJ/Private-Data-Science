# Concept Inventories — Overview, Status & Descriptions

Compiled 2026-07-03 from `concept-inventories.pdf` (ASBMB catalog of concept inventories across biology, chemistry, math, physics, and astronomy).

**Companion file:** the actual question items are in `2026-07-03-concept-inventories-question-sets.md`. This file is the index — the table, the explanation of availability, and a description of every instrument with its status.

## What the source PDF is

The source PDF is a **catalog/bibliography**, not a question bank. It names ~65 instruments and gives references and, in some cases, a URL — but it contains no actual test items. This file records the result of trying to locate the **actual question set** for each.

## The main finding

Most of these instruments are **deliberately access-restricted**. Their developers distribute them only to verified educators (via PhysPort or ComPADRE), require you to "contact the author," or publish them behind journal paywalls. In many cases the developers explicitly ask that items **not be redistributed or posted publicly**, because open circulation destroys the test's diagnostic validity. So for the majority, the honest answer is: *the set exists and is obtainable through the proper channel, but is not openly reproducible.*

A minority were published openly (a journal appendix, a CC-BY open-access supplement, or a "test-yourself" quiz). Of those, three extract cleanly enough to reproduce verbatim and are pasted in the companion file (**DODT, Basic Tree Thinking, ADT**). The remaining open ones are one-click open-access but their PDFs only extract as garbled OCR — pasting that into a benchmark dataset would introduce errors, so they are given as direct open links instead (and can be transcribed on request).

**Status legend:**

- **✅ FULL SET — pasted** in the companion file (clean, openly published).
- **✅ Local** — you already have it in your `FCI/` folder.
- **🔓 Open link** — full instrument is freely available at a public/open-access URL; linked rather than pasted (OCR not clean, or author asks items not be redistributed).
- **🔒 Gated** — PhysPort / ComPADRE / password / "contact the author."
- **💰 Paywalled** — items exist only inside a paywalled journal article/appendix.
- **❌ Not found** — no accessible full instrument located.

## Descriptions & status by discipline

### Biochemistry & Molecular Biology

- **Expected Biology & Chemistry Foundational Concepts** (Villafañe et al., 2011) — a multiple-choice instrument measuring five general-chemistry and three biology concepts considered prerequisite for biochemistry. 💰 Items in the paywalled BAMBED / CERP articles (DOI 10.1002/bmb.20464).
- **Enzyme–Substrate Interactions Concept Inventory (ESICI)** (Bretz & Linenberger, 2012) — 15 items on enzyme–substrate interactions. 🔒 Bretz's instruments are released only on request to instructors.
- **Molecular Life Sciences Concept Inventory (MLSCI / MLS)** (Howitt, Anderson, et al.) — assesses dynamic equilibrium, macromolecular structure, energy transformations, information coding, catalysis, cellular compartmentalization. 🔒 Online modules, instructor registration.
- **Taxonomy of Biochemistry External Representations (TOBER)** (Towns et al.) — not a student test: a scheme for classifying the external representations used in biochemistry teaching. n/a.

### Biology (Molecular/Cell, Genetics)

- **Biology Concept Inventory (BCI)** (Klymkowsky & Garvin-Doxas) — 30 questions on general biology concepts. 🔒 On request via bioliteracy.colorado.edu.
- **Introductory Molecular & Cell Biology Assessment (IMCA)** (Shi et al., 2010) — 24 MC items, diagrams. 🔓 Full items in the open-access CBE-LSE 9(4):453 supplemental material.
- **Osmosis & Diffusion Conceptual Assessment (ODCA)** (Fisher, Williams & Lineback, 2011) — 8 two-tier items; incorporates parts of the DODT. 🔓 Open-access CBE-LSE 10(4):418 supplement.
- **Meiosis Concept Inventory** (Kalas et al., 2013) — 17 mixed MC / select-all items. 🔒 Via q4b.biology.ubc.ca to registered instructors.
- **Genetics Literacy Assessment Instrument (GLAI)** (Bowling et al., 2008) — 31 items on genetics and inheritance. 🔓/💰 Reproduced in the Moskalik 2007 M.S. thesis (OhioLINK, accession ucin1195583851).
- **Genetics Concept Assessment (GCA)** (Smith, Wood & Knight, 2008) — 25 MC items. 🔒 On request from the authors.
- **Genetics Diagnostic Instrument** (Tsui & Treagust, 2009) — 13 two-tier items. 💰 Paywalled IJSE article.
- **Developmental Biology Content Survey** (Knight & Wood, 2005) — 15 MC items. 🔓 Open-access Cell Biology Education 4(4):298.
- **Host-Pathogen Interactions (HPI)** (Marbach-Ad et al., 2009/2010) — 17–18 two-tier items. 💰 Paywalled JMBE articles.
- **Breathing & Respiration** (Mann & Treagust, 1998) — 12 two-tier items. 💰 Australian Science Teachers Journal 44:55.
- **Photosynthesis & Respiration** (Haslam & Treagust, 1987) — 13 two-tier items plus open-ended. 💰 J. Biological Education 21:203.
- **Flowering Plant Growth & Development** (Lin, 2004) — 13 two-tier items. 💰 IJSME 2:175 (Springer).
- **Internal Transport in Plants & Human Circulatory System** (Wang, 2004) — 28 two-tier items, three grade-level versions. 💰 IJSME 2:131 (Springer).

### Evolution & Ecology

- **Conceptual Inventory of Natural Selection (CINS)** (Anderson, Fisher & Norman, 2002) — 20 MC items built on real scientific studies, using misconceptions as distractors. 🔒 On request via Fisher's group; appendix of the paywalled JRST article.
- **Natural Selection instrument** (Nehm & Reilly, 2007) — 6 open-response prompts. 💰 Inside the BioScience 57:263 article.
- **Measure of Understanding of Macroevolution (MUM)** (Nadelson & Southerland, 2010) — 27 MC + 1 free-response. 💰 Paywalled J. Experimental Education 78:151.
- **Phylogeny Assessment Tool (PhAT)** (Smith, Cheruvelil & Auvenshine, 2013) — three-part instrument, 3 open-ended questions. 🔓 Open-access in PMC3763020.
- **Genetic Drift Inventory (GeDI)** (Price et al., 2014) — 22 agree/disagree items across four scenarios, testing four key concepts and six misconceptions. 🔓 Open-access (CC-BY) CBE-LSE 13(1):65. (Branching scenario structure — see note in the companion file.)
- **EvoDevo Concept Inventory (EvoDevoCI)** (Perez et al., 2013) — 11 MC items, 4 scenarios. 🔓 Open-access (CC-BY) CBE-LSE 12(4):665 supplement.
- **Diagnostic Question Clusters on Energy & Matter (DQCs)** (Wilson et al., 2006) — 16 clusters of ~7 items each. 🔒 Download after registration at biodqc.org.

### Chemistry

- **Chemistry Competence Test (CCT)** (Potgieter & Davidowitz, 2011) — diagnostic of entering-student proficiency. 💰 CERP 12:193.
- **Chemistry Reasoning Test (CRT)** (Cloonan & Hutchinson, 2011) — conceptual understanding and critical scientific thinking. 💰 CERP 12:205.
- **Chemistry Concept Inventory (ChCI)** (Birk et al., 2004) — 60 items across two semesters (thermochemistry, bonding, IMF, equilibrium, acids/bases, electrochemistry). 🔒 Contact Jim Birk, ASU.
- **Acid-strength alternative-conceptions diagnostic** (McClary & Bretz, 2012) — organic-chemistry acid strength. 💰 IJSE 34(15).

### Thermodynamics / Quantum Mechanics (listed under Chemistry and Physics)

- **Thermodynamics Concept Inventory (TCI)** (Midkiff et al., 2001) — intro thermodynamics. 🔒 Contact Clark Midkiff, Alabama.
- **Introductory Thermal Concept Evaluation (ITCE)** (Yeo & Zadnik, 2001) — thermal concepts for 15–18 year olds. 💰 The Physics Teacher 39.
- **Quantum Mechanics Visualization Instrument (QMVI)** (Cataloglu/Robinett) — core intro QM concepts. 🔒 Contact Richard Robinett, Penn State.
- **Quantum Mechanics Survey (QMS)** (Singh & Zhu) — junior-level QM in one dimension. 🔒 Password via perusersguide.org.
- **Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey (QMCS)** (McKagan, Perkins & Wieman, 2010) — 12 items. 🔒 Via PhysPort / on request; the open PRPER paper describes but does not print all items.
- **Quantum Mechanics Assessment Tool (QMAT)** (Goldhaber, Pollock et al., 2009) — upper-division QM. 🔒 Contact Steve Pollock, Colorado.

### Math

- **Calculus Concept Inventory (CCI)** (Epstein) — basic differential-calculus principles. 🔒 Restricted; contact author.
- **Precalculus Concept Assessment (PCA)** (Carlson, Oehrtman & Engelke, 2010) — 25 items on rate of change, function, covariational reasoning. 🔒 On request via mathed.asu.edu.
- **Math Skills Inventory** — a 34-item self-assessment of study behaviors (not a concept test). 🔓 Publicly posted (UAF; the page is JS-rendered — open in a browser).
- **Quadratic & Linear Conceptual Evaluation (QLCE)** — 40 items on quadratic modeling and graph/equation relationships. 🔒 Formerly on physics.dickinson.edu; now restricted. No publication identified.
- **Vector Evaluation Test (VET)** — 31 items on vector addition/subtraction, components, magnitudes. 🔒 Formerly on physics.dickinson.edu; now restricted. No publication identified.

- **Energy & Momentum Conceptual Survey (EMCS)** (Singh & Rosengrant, 2003) — energy and momentum. 🔒 ComPADRE ID 11896.
- **Energy Concept Assessment (ECA)** (Ding) — energy topics for calculus-based intro physics. 🔒 Contact Lin Ding, Ohio State.
- **Heat & Energy Concept Inventory (HECI)** (Prince, Vigeant & Nottis, 2012) — 36 items. 💰 J. Engineering Education 101(3).
- **Heat & Temperature Concept Evaluation (HTCE)** (Thornton & Sokoloff, 1987) — 28 items on heat, temperature, heat flow. 🔒 Formerly on physics.dickinson.edu; restricted.
- **Heat Transfer Concept Inventory (HTCI)** (Martin, Mitchell & Newell, 2003) — conduction, convection, radiation. 🔒 Contact Jay Martin, UW-Madison.
- **Thermal & Transport Concept Inventory (TTCI)** (Streveler, Miller et al., 2011) — heat transfer, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics for engineers. 🔒 Contact Ron Miller, Colorado School of Mines.
- **Waves Concept Inventory (WCI)** (Roedel et al., 1998) — wave visualization, math depiction, definitions. 🔒 Contact Ron Roedel, ASU.
- **Wave Diagnostic Test (WDT)** (Redish, 1999) — intro wave physics difficulties. 🔒 Contact Janice Redish, Maryland.
- **Circuits Concept Inventory (CCI)** (Helgeland & Rancour, 2013) — DC/AC circuits and frequency-domain concepts. 🔒 Foundation Coalition; access restricted.
- **Electromagnetics (EMCI)** (Notaros, 2002) — fields, waves, and combined. 🔒 Contact Branislav Notaros.
- **Electronics Concept Inventory (ECI)** (Simoni, Herniter & Ferguson, 2004) — 35 items on introductory electronics. 🔒 Contact Marc Herniter, Rose-Hulman.

### Astronomy

- **Astronomy Diagnostic Test (ADT) 2.0** (Hufnagel / CAER, 1999) — 33 questions (21 content + 12 demographic). ✅ Pasted in the companion file. **Note:** the CAER authors ask that items not be handed out to students; it is included here for your research use — treat it as restricted.
- **Astronomical Misconceptions Survey (AMS)** (LoPresto & Murrell, 2011) — intro-course misconceptions. 💰 JCST 40(5).
- **Newtonian Gravity Concept Inventory (NGCI)** (Williamson, Willoughby & Prather, 2013). 🔒 PhysPort.
- **Lunar Phases Concept Inventory (LPCI)** (Lindell & Olsen, 2002) — mental models of lunar phases. 🔒 Contact Rebecca Lindell.
- **Star Properties Concept Inventory (SPCI)** (Bailey et al.) — stellar mass, temperature, luminosity, lifetime, fusion, star formation. 🔒 Contact author.
- **Space Physics Concept Inventory** (Doxas et al., 2005) — ❌ only a 2005 AGU meeting abstract; no evidence a full instrument was released.

### Nature of Science

- **Views of the Nature of Science (VNOS)** (Lederman et al., 2002) — open-ended prompts on the empirical, tentative, inferential, creative, and social nature of science. 🔓 VNOS-B and VNOS-C hosted at flaguide.org (JS-rendered; open in a browser).
- **Views on Science & Education (VOSE)** (Chen, 2006) — attitudes toward and knowledge of the nature of science. 💰 Instrument in the appendix of Science Education 90:803. The source PDF notes VNOS/VOSE are not technically concept inventories.
