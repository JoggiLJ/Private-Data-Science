# Force Concept Inventory (FCI)

**Source:** Hestenes, Wells & Swackhamer (1992), with the widely-used v95 revision by Halloun, Hake, Mosca & Hestenes.

**What it tests:** Conceptual understanding of Newtonian mechanics (force, motion, inertia), specifically designed to distinguish genuine Newtonian reasoning from persistent Aristotelian/common-sense misconceptions about how forces and motion work. One of the original and most influential "concept inventories" in physics education research.

**Format:** 30 multiple-choice items, each option engineered to correspond to a specific known misconception (not just plausible-but-wrong distractors).

**What's in this folder:** Two source PDFs (`FCI-v95-en (1).pdf` and `forced-cocept-inventory.pdf`) and `FCI_JSON/` with the transcribed question set.
